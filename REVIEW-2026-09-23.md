# Dataset & pipeline review, 2026-09-23 — THREE INDEPENDENT AUDITS

Triggered by Mingyou after the D3 prompt-collision incident. Four self-run
lenses + three independent agent audits (semantic validity, adversarial
shortcut hunt, code correctness). VERDICT: the dataset cannot support RQ3 as
built, and three harness/adapter bugs of ours contaminated already-reported
findings. Full rebuild required before any further inference.

## FATAL — dataset construction

F1. FORMAT TELL. Surface-feature logistic regression separates FAR from NEAR
    options at AUC 0.802 clinc / 0.940 goemotions / 0.903 fintopic. A
    TEXT-FREE gold picker (no access to the input) reaches 0.72 accuracy at
    K=8 goemotions/far vs 0.125 chance; format lift at clinc K=64 is +0.175,
    the same order as the Laya Delta (+0.235) we reported. Root cause is
    twofold: norm() concatenated 92/537 labels (17-24% of served FAR
    distractors), and source composition (ledgar+dbpedia = 32% of universe,
    structurally FAR-only).

F2. GOLD POSITION IS A FUNCTION OF THE GOLD LABEL (ours, stage2.py:136).
    seed = crc32(gold+tier)+K omits the item index, so every item sharing a
    gold sits at the same position. goemotions/far K=256 used 24 of 64
    positions, 75 items on one. Measured position effect on Laya: per-position
    accuracy spread 0.125 (goemotions K=16) to 0.500 (fintopic K=64).
    Bootstrap CIs are therefore too narrow — the direction that manufactures
    "CI excludes 0" for C2/C3. Order runs use per-item seeds and are unaffected.

F3. EXCLUSION FILTER IS FAMILY-BIASED. mpnet+e5 delete 83-87% of bge/gte's
    top non-gold competitor but only 49% of deberta's (random expectation 3%).
    The prereg controlled filter-model IDENTITY, not FAMILY; mpnet/e5 compute
    exactly what EmbeddingSim computes. Contaminates the headline cross-family
    comparison.

F4. FINTOPIC NEAR IS SELECTION-BIASED AND UNPAIRED. 97/200 feasible at K=16;
    survivors' cos(text,gold) 0.213 vs dropped 0.315 (p=9.5e-10); leakage 2%
    vs 18%; three gold classes (markets, dividend, ipo) dropped entirely.
    acc_far(200 items, 18 classes) - acc_near(97 items, 15 classes) is a
    class-mix contrast, not a tier contrast.

F5. TIERS DON'T SEPARATE ON CLINC (ours). Source-based NEAR vs FAR mean
    gold-distractor cosine: clinc +0.007, goemotions +0.187, fintopic +0.119.
    CLINC-150 is multi-domain, so "sibling" != "semantically near"
    ('pto balance' vs 'change ai name' = 0.013). CLINC was the only domain
    where C2's three-K rule applied.

F6. GOEMOTIONS NEAR IS NOT SINGLE-ANSWER. 52% of near-tier items offer a
    distractor that a real GoEmotions rater voted for on that exact text
    (measured from the raw config, no judgment involved); 48% strict
    ambiguity on read; ~8% of golds wrong. 'neutral' is 37.5% of golds and is
    a residual rater-agreement category, not a positive claim about the text.

## FATAL — our adapters / harness

F7. LAYA OPTION RENDERING DOUBLES EVERY LABEL (models.py:139). criteria={o:o}
    renders "transfer: transfer", doubling option tokens. At K=256 this
    OVERFLOWS the budget: 1764 option tokens vs 882 for bare list; 10 of 256
    options collapse into 4 indistinguishable stubs; instruction cut to 8
    tokens. Our marker-count guard never fires because the COUNT stays 256 —
    the CONTENT is truncated. Switching to bare rendering changes the argmax
    on 13/40 items.
    => THE REPORTED LAYA "CONTEXT CEILING" AT K=256 IS PARTLY OUR BUG.
    Every Laya K>=128 number since wave 2 is suspect, pilot included.

F8. JSONL ROUNDING RECREATES THE ARTIFACT WE FIXED (stage2.py:194).
    round(p, 6) per-item; Laya K=256 loses the gold probability to 0.0 on
    8/200 items -> NLL 27.6 instead of the real value. In-run stats are
    correct; the per-item files that every CI/calibration analysis reads are
    not. This is the same class of defect as agent.predict()'s 4-decimal
    rounding that motivated the direct-forward rewrite.

F9. LADDER DOESN'T NEST FOR FINTOPIC (stage2.py:75). fintopic validation has
    4117 rows; random.Random(101).sample(list, 500) crosses CPython's
    sample-algorithm boundary, so only 40 of the 200 items recur at n=500.
    The prereg's 200->500 stopping rule assumes nesting.

## SERIOUS

S1. assert_no_collision CANNOT FIRE (ours). PROMPT_STOP was built from the
    prompt's own content words, so the checked set is always empty. D3's
    third remediation step does not do what it claims.
S2. D3 MISATTRIBUTION (ours). GLiClassZS.decide accepts hypothesis_template
    and question and USES NEITHER — it passes bare option strings. GLiClass
    was immune to the 'text' collision because it never saw the prompt, not
    because of architectural robustness. The "robustness difference worth
    reporting" claim in D3 is withdrawn.
S3. CONFLICT SUBTRACTION IN NEAR IS A NO-OP. Nominations are cross-source
    only, so no conflict partner can be in a same-source near pool: measured
    0 removals from NEAR in all three domains (85/27/170 from FAR). The
    prereg's NEAR definition promises de-confusion the pipeline cannot deliver.
S4. CONFLICT BANS LAND 100% ON FAR, giving FAR a semantic floor NEAR lacks:
    items with a distractor at cos>=0.60 to gold: FAR 0.0% everywhere,
    NEAR 3.0% clinc / 36.5% goemotions / 0.0% fintopic.
S5. 25-35% OF FAR DISTRACTORS COME FROM A1'S DISCLOSED TRAINING LABEL SETS
    (banking77, massive, ag_news) vs 0-2.7% in NEAR. Rejecting a label you
    were trained on is easier; helps A1 in FAR only.
S6. NEAR DEGENERATES TO A CLOSED SET. Mean pairwise Jaccard of served option
    sets: fintopic near 0.681, goemotions near 0.409 vs far ~0.019. NEAR is
    the familiar closed-set task; FAR is fresh each item.
S7. EXCLUSION EATS NEAR MUCH HARDER THAN FAR: 4.8% / 10.0% / 23.7% of the
    near pool vs ~2% of far — and it removes the text-nearest, i.e. hardest,
    siblings. Biases Delta toward zero, oppositely to S4/S5. Net sign unknown.
S8. MIXED IS NOT INTERMEDIATE. Items whose 16-option mixed set has ZERO
    same-source distractors: clinc 0%, goemotions 46%, fintopic 67%.
S9. SILENT-FAILURE TRAPS. A cell where all decide() calls raise is logged
    identically to an infeasible cell (fails key dropped when n==0). Failed
    order permutations are skipped without a counter, biasing flip rates
    DOWN — the direction that makes C1's "<5%" rule easier to pass.
S10. GOLD LEAKAGE GROWS WITH K. Verbatim gold in text: clinc 24%, fintopic
    10.5%. Laya clinc/far leaked-vs-not accuracy gap: +0.00 (K=2) -> +0.271
    (K=256). The leaked quarter has a near-flat K-curve, so CLINC's pooled
    curve shape depends on the leakage mix.
S11. RUNGS.PY SILENTLY UPGRADES CLINC TO G4* FOR LAYA (ours). FAMILY is keyed
    on wave-1 dataset names; stage-2 domain names miss, falling through to the
    partial-disclosure branch. The prereg requires G3 there. gte-large is
    absent from MODELS and grades UG.
S12. 4 OF 16 DECLARED SOURCES NEVER LOADED (mtop, hwu64, trec_fine, yahoo) —
    caught only by a log line. Universe is 537, not the prereg's "600+".

## VERIFIED CORRECT (do not re-audit)

- Adapter index alignment, all four models: rotating options through every
  position, 0/10 misalignments; position-aligned control deviates 0.40-1.00,
  so a mis-indexed adapter would have been caught.
- GLiClass sigmoid inversion is exact (single-label softmax and multi-label
  sigmoid share logits); eps clamp never activates; no label truncation at
  K=256; all 256 labels returned.
- Laya direct-forward math matches agent.system_one line for line (clamped
  temperature table, max-subtracted softmax, autocast off on MPS).
- build_pools seeding: 1800 distinct seeds for 1800 draws, no collisions,
  no overlap with order-seed range.
- Tier purity: zero violations both directions; the 21 multi-source options
  behave correctly.
- Alias handling: no alias survives as a key, no canonical missing, no
  conflict endpoint is an alias; gold never in its own distractor list.
- Pilot-item exclusion from the CLINC draw is exact (0 leaks).
- TWITTER_FIN_TOPICS id->name mapping verified against sample texts, all 20.
- Order-run seeds are collision-free; all 5 permutations distinct for all 600
  items at K=16/far.
- Post-reword, Laya's clinc/far errors are diffuse (no wrong pick repeated
  >4 times) — the D3 'text' defect is genuinely closed.

## WHAT SURVIVES FROM PREVIOUSLY REPORTED FINDINGS

- Order sensitivity is REAL and architectural: independent test shows Laya's
  argmax changes on 9/48 shuffles at K=64 and 23/48 at K=128; bge and deberta
  are exactly permutation-equivariant (max deviation 1.2e-07). The CLASS
  distinction stands. The specific RATES are biased low by S9.
- Calibration: "no transferable temperature" stands (within-model T spread).
- GLiClass raw-distribution fix stands (verified exact).
- The K-curve DIRECTION (accuracy falls with K) stands for all models, but
  Laya's magnitude at K>=128 is confounded by F7.

## REBUILD PLAN (nothing runs until all gates pass)

1. universe v3: CamelCase/dot-aware normalization; make source failures fatal;
   record realised manifest in the JSON; same-source nomination + adjudication.
2. Tiers by MEASURED similarity band + SURFACE-MATCHED far sampling.
   Validated: format AUC 0.656/0.818/0.845 -> 0.547/0.604/0.635; separation
   +0.007/+0.187/+0.119 -> +0.243/+0.246/+0.258; K=64 near feasible
   200/0/0 -> 200/200/200 per domain.
3. Adapters: bare-list Laya rendering (F7); full-precision JSONL (F8);
   GLiClass to receive the prompt or be documented as prompt-free (S2).
4. Seeds: item-indexed positions (F2); explicit nesting for the ladder (F9).
5. Filters: family-balanced ensemble or drop per-item exclusion for
   adjudication (F3); rank-based within-tier so both tiers lose equal share (S7).
6. GoEmotions: rater-attested alternatives excluded per item, or domain
   dropped from the near tier (F6).
7. Gates as PRE-INFERENCE assertions: format AUC <= 0.60; text-free gold
   picker <= 2x chance; gold-position uniformity chi-square; per-tier
   gold-distractor cosine distribution logged; PROMPT_STOP from a fixed
   function-word list so the collision check can actually fail (S1).
8. Analysis: leakage-stratified K-curves (S10); intersection-only tier
   contrasts (F4); rungs.py keyed to stage-2 domains (S11).

# REBUILD EXECUTED (same day)

universe_v3: 802 options from 12 loaded sources (6 declared sources failed and
are RECORDED in the manifest, not silently dropped), 430 conflicts of which 20
are explicit ambiguity bans from the semantic audit, 84 same-source pairs kept
as legitimate near-tier difficulty, 7 hypernym magnets removed.

Fixes applied and verified by gate:
- normalization: CamelCase/dot-aware split before lowercase. Concatenated
  labels 92 -> 18 (the 18 are genuinely long single words).
- tiers: MEASURED gold-similarity bands, not source membership; FAR sampled
  one-for-one into NEAR's surface strata, taking the LEAST similar option in
  each stratum.
- position: seeded on the item uid, not the gold label.
- same-source adjudication: cross-source defaults to conflict, same-source
  defaults to KEEP with an explicit ambiguity list (high cosine != ambiguous:
  'iot hue lighton' vs 'lightoff' are opposite actions and are exactly the
  difficulty the near tier should contain).
- GoEmotions: per-item exclusion of every rater-attested alternative from the
  raw config (302 exclusions across 200 items).
- text filter: rank-based, equal FRACTION per tier (was a fixed 10 per item,
  costing NEAR up to 23.7% of its pool and FAR 2%).
- K ceiling for the tier contrast set to 64 and justified: 63 distractors is
  12% of the universe and can still be called near; 255 would be 32% and could
  not. The K-curve beyond 64 runs on the far tier only.

GATE RESULTS: 7/8 pass.
  G1 prompt collision        PASS (guard now uses a fixed function-word list,
                                   so it can actually fail)
  G2 format tell             PASS max AUC 0.573 (was 0.940)
  G3 text-free gold picker   FAIL max 0.280 (fintopic/far; gate <=0.125)
  G4 gold position uniform   PASS 60-63 of 64 slots used, max share 0.04
                                   (was 24 of 64 slots, 75 items on one)
  G5 tier separation         PASS +0.211 to +0.238 (was +0.007 on clinc)
  G6 pairing feasible        PASS every item in both tiers at every K
                                   (was 0/200 near at K=64 for two domains)
  G7 no conflict in options  PASS
  G8 gold present once       PASS

THE ONE OPEN GATE, stated honestly. A classifier with NO access to the item
text still locates the gold above 2x chance in some cells, worst at
fintopic/far (0.280 vs 0.0625 chance). Cause: fin-topic's labels have a
distinctive surface grammar ('gold or metals or materials', 'treasuries or
corporate debt') that no natural label universe populates densely, so the far
tier cannot be matched into those strata. Two remedies were TRIED AND
REJECTED, both recorded:
  - coarser strata: raises supply but reopens G2 (fintopic AUC 0.681).
  - dropping thin-stratum items: costs 90 of 200 fintopic items AND makes G3
    worse, because the survivors are a format-typical subsample.
Adopted instead: keep all items, disclose the residual, and report the
text-free picker accuracy per cell beside every RQ3 table so readers can
discount the affected cells. fintopic's FAR tier carries the largest residual
and its tier contrast should be read with that in mind, or dropped from C2.
